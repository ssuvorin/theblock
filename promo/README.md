# Promo

`career-brain-promo.mp4` — the 30-second promo. 1920×1080, H.264 / AAC, 21 MB.

Cut from the dark-theme frames in [`../mockups/design/`](../mockups/design). The
narrative it follows is [`DEMO-FLOW.md`](../DEMO-FLOW.md): the question, the ranked
answer, the introduction path lighting up, the draft, the close.

Voice and visual rules it has to stay inside are in [`../DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md) —
in particular: Safety Orange is the only saturated colour, and promo frames sit at
`#0A0B0D` rather than pure black, because video compression eats crushed blacks.
